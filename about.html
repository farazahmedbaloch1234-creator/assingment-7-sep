About the Institute :

saylani welfare international trust is a non-profile orgaization in pakistan. it provides education ,vocational training,healthcare,food,andother services to help people in need.



Mission

Saylani Welfare International Trust's mission is to serve humanity across every stage of life by alleviating suffering, nurturing self-sufficiency, and bringing hope and dignity to millions worldwide through essential welfare initiatives.

Institute vision

Saylani Welfare International Trust's core institutional vision is “To create a compassionate world with equal opportunities for all

short description

Saylani Welfare International Trust is one of Pakistan’s largest non-governmental organizations (NGOs), dedicated to breaking the cycle of poverty and uplifting the impoverished. Founded in May 1999 by the spiritual and religious scholar Maulana Bashir Ahmed Farooqui, the charity is headquartered in Karachi and operates hundreds of branches globally. “The organization started out as a rented house and today has at least 630 branches worldwide.
